Common questions

Straight answers, no sales copy.

Why not charge a % of ad spend like other agencies?
Because it creates the wrong incentive. Percentage-of-spend agencies make more money when you spend more. I'm paid a flat fee, so my incentive is to get you the best results with the budget you have — not to increase your spend.
What happens after the free 60 days?
We review results together. If the campaigns are working and you want ongoing management, we discuss a paid arrangement (typically the Managed plan). There's no pressure — if you want to take over the account yourself, I'll hand it off clean and documented.
How is this different from hiring an agency?
You're working directly with me, not an account manager who manages 30+ clients. I have 3 years of Google Ads experience at a publicly-traded Canadian company. Smaller client roster = more attention per account.
What's the minimum ad budget I need?
For the free trial, any budget works — I've seen great results at $500/month. For paid management, I recommend at least $1,000/month in ad spend so the results can justify the management fee.
Do you work with businesses outside Ontario?
Not right now. I'm focused on building an Ontario-specific portfolio of results. Once those are established, I'll expand. If you're outside Ontario, sign up for the newsletter — I'll announce it there first.
