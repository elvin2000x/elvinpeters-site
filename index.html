About

I help professionals stop drowning in work and start leveraging AI like a team of one.

I spent years running paid media for some of Canada's biggest brands — first as a Google Ads strategist, then managing millions in ad spend at goeasy, one of Canada's largest consumer finance companies.

When I went self-employed, I had a simple problem: I needed to do the work of a full team, alone. AI solved that. Not perfectly, not without learning — but decisively.

Now I build AI-automated businesses and document everything: the tools, the workflows, the prompts, and the systems that actually work. That became a book. Then a newsletter. Then this.

Based in Toronto. Moving fast.
